Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. Applies to the Fish app for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

What Fish does not collect

Fish does not use advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking. We do not sell personal data. There are no user accounts inside the app.

Where your vocabulary lives

Entries, scores, comments and media filenames are stored on your device and, if you are signed in with iCloud, in your iCloud (CloudKit and iCloud Drive). Apple provides that storage under your Apple ID. The developer cannot read your cards from iCloud.

Photos

Fish asks for photo library access only so you can attach a mnemo image to a vocabulary card. Images stay in your Fish media folder (on-device and, when available, iCloud Drive). They are not uploaded to our servers.

Audio

Imported audio files stay in the same media folder. Listener uses the device audio session so drills can continue with the screen locked. That is playback on your device, not recording or uploading your voice.

Settings on the device

Session filters and the optional backup-folder bookmark are stored in Apple UserDefaults on the device. They are not sent to us.

Optional Dropbox / Files backup

If you choose a folder in the Files app (for example inside Dropbox), Fish copies a disaster-recovery folder there. Access is limited to the folder you pick. We do not use the Dropbox SDK and we do not receive a copy of that backup.

Children

Fish does not target children for data collection. Anyone can use the practice modes with their own word list.
